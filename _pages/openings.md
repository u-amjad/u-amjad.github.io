# Join Us

We welcome motivated students interested in machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, medical image analysis, and intelligent systems.

## Applications for PhD

If you are from computer science, artificial intelligence, or a related engineering discipline and are interested in working as a PhD student on machine learning, deep learning and computer vision or medical image analysis, please send an email to [usmanamjad@neduet.edu.pk](mailto:usmanamjad@neduet.edu.pk). State briefly why you are interested and attach a CV, including your previous and pending publications.

Use **"Application PhD"** as the subject line.

## Applications for MS research students

If you are an MS student interested in machine learning, deep learning and computer vision, please send an email describing your interests along with your CV and academic transcripts.

Use **"Application MS Research"** as the subject line.

## Applications for Bachelor's students

If you are a Bachelor's student looking for a final year project or research experience in our areas, send a short email describing what interests you, along with your CV and transcript.

Use **"Application BS Research"** as the subject line.
