# Research

Our research focuses on developing intelligent, reliable, and efficient AI systems for real-world challenges, with a growing emphasis on healthcare. Over more than a decade, our work has evolved from fuzzy reasoning and nature-inspired optimization to deep learning, computer vision, and graph-based AI.

A key theme across our research is that strong AI models require more than architecture alone. We explore intelligent optimization, parameter tuning, and robust validation to improve model performance while maintaining practical efficiency. Our work has investigated methods including Genetic Algorithms, Particle Swarm Optimization, Grasshopper, Firefly, and Grey Wolf Optimization.

Our primary research direction is now AI in healthcare, particularly computer vision and data-driven systems for reliable medical decision support. This includes work on brain tumor classification, cancer driver gene prediction, Alzheimer’s detection, and lightweight tumor detection, alongside optimized deep-learning models such as ResNet, DenseNet, EfficientNet, and Xception.

Our current work further focuses on AI-based wound monitoring and measurement, using computer vision to support automated wound assessment and progression monitoring in clinical settings.

Across these areas, our goal is to develop AI systems that are accurate, efficient, trustworthy, and practical, especially in real-world and resource-constrained healthcare environments.
