# Research

Our research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence, computer vision, medical AI, intelligent systems, edge and IoT AI, and optimization. We develop computational methods for medical image analysis, disease detection and characterization, and the analysis of complex biomedical data, alongside resource-efficient AI systems for edge and IoT environments. An emerging direction of our work focuses on multimodal and biologically informed AI, integrating medical imaging with other biomedical data to build context-aware models that support more accurate and interpretable disease assessment.
