## One-Day Training on "Understanding Data Biases and Mitigation Strategies"

*Pakistan Audit & Accounts Academy (PAAA), Karachi — 17 March 2026*

Faculty members from the Department of Computer Science & Information Technology, NED University, contributed to public sector capacity building by delivering a one-day training on "Understanding Data Biases and Mitigation Strategies" at the Pakistan Audit & Accounts Academy (PAAA), Karachi. Dr. Waseemullah Nazir and **Dr. Usman Amjad** shared their expertise, supporting government institutions in leveraging AI for improved efficiency and informed decision-making, reflecting CSIT's commitment to societal impact and digital transformation.

## One-Day Training on "Data Literacy & Analytics"

*Pakistan Audit & Accounts Academy (PAAA), Karachi — 24 November 2025*

Faculty members from the Department of Computer Science & Information Technology, NED University of Engineering & Technology, contributed to public sector capacity building by delivering a one-day short course on "Data Literacy & Analytics" at the Pakistan Audit & Accounts Academy (PAAA), Karachi. **Dr. Usman Amjad** designed and conducted the training sessions, equipping officers and officials with essential knowledge of data literacy, data interpretation, and practical analytics techniques relevant to audit and public sector functions. Through this initiative, the CSIT faculty continued to support professional development in the public sector, reflecting the department's commitment to advancing digital transformation, data-driven governance, and societal impact through knowledge sharing and academic excellence.
